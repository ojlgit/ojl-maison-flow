# 08 - Art Direction and Material Rules

## Visual target

Premium OJL luxury gallery: quiet, precise, material-rich, cinematic.

Use:

- dark lacquer
- warm champagne metal
- ivory stone
- smoked glass
- brushed metal
- leather grain
- warm display lighting
- reflective plinths
- arch/alcove geometry
- soft contact shadows

Avoid:

- neon gaming style
- cartoon jewelry
- random particles
- overly fast rotation
- cheap mirror reflections everywhere
- over-bright bloom
- generic SaaS gradients
- copied Cartier designs or watch faces

## Jewelry material rules

Gold:

- high metalness
- controlled roughness
- warm color
- environment reflections are critical
- do not make it flat yellow

Diamonds/gems:

- use simplified transmissive physical materials first
- add sparkle with restraint
- avoid fake rainbow overload
- custom shader only after baseline works

Glass:

- use transmission/opacity carefully
- smoked glass should be subtle
- avoid sorting-heavy overlapping glass when possible

Stone:

- use subtle noise and roughness
- ivory/warm gallery materials should support jewelry, not overpower it

Leather:

- use normal/roughness texture
- keep scale believable

## Lighting rules

- Jewelry needs environment lighting.
- Room architecture can use baked-looking light.
- Hero products need strong but soft key lights.
- Use contact shadows to anchor objects.
- Avoid too many dynamic shadow-casting lights.

## Motion rules

- Product motion should be slow and intentional.
- Camera path should feel like a directed luxury film.
- Avoid sudden movement unless hidden by a portal/transition.
- Camera should focus on products, not empty rooms.

## UI rules

- UI should be minimal and premium.
- Copy should not cover the jewelry.
- CTAs must be available but quiet.
- The experience must remain usable without sound.
